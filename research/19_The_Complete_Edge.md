## 1. Model-ka: **D – 7H – 1H Alignment**
Casharku wuxuu bixinayaa model saddex heer ah: **Bias (D) → Confirmation (7H) → Entry (1H)**.

## 2. D BIAS
Daily bias waxaa lagu go'aamiyaa:
- **Candle type**
- **Drawn on LQ (DOL) / Key level**
- **Invalidation Level**

### 2.1 D Reversal scenarios
- **Bearish reversal:** candle 1 (bullish) wuxuu gaaraa **KL / MAJOR LQ** (kor). Candle 2 (bearish) wuxuu qaadaa high-ga candle 1 (sweep), kadib wuxuu dib ugu xirmaa gudaha. Target: **DOL** = low-ga candle 1.
- **Bullish reversal:** waa tan ka soo horjeedda. Candle 2 wuxuu qaadaa low-ga candle 1 ee ku yaal **KL / MAJOR LQ**, target-kuna waa **DOL** = high-ga candle 1.

### 2.2 D Continuation scenarios
- KL/major LQ, kadib candle 2 ayaa sameeya reversal yar, kadibna candles 3 iyo 4 ayaa sii wada jihada (**Contin**). Tani waa continuation ka dambeeya reversal-ka.
- Tusaalaha bullish: 1 → 2 (inside/retracement) → 3 → 3 (contin), oo ah close above.
- Tani waxay la jaanqaadaysaa casharka 04 (Next Candle Expectation: close above/below = continuation).
