## 1. Daily Profiles — qeexid  [SLIDE]
> "Daily Profiles waa mid ka mid ah concept-yada ugu muhiimsan trading-ka, sababtoo ah waxay kaa caawinayaan inaad fahanto sida market-ku u dhaqaaqo maalin kasta iyo sida sessions-ku isku raacaan. Suuqu si random ah uma socdo. Daily Profiles waxay trader-ka siinayaan roadmap uu ku aqoonsado High of the Day (HOD), Low of the Day (LOD), direction-ka maalinta, iyo sida loo heli lahaa entries tayo sare leh."

## 2. Waqtiga  [AUDIO+]
- **Dhammaan waqtiyadu waa New York time** [P1 05:00–05:06].
- Maalinta (daily candle) waxay furantaa **5 PM NY**, taas oo ah saqda dhexe (12:00) waqtiga Soomaaliya / East Africa [P1 05:23–05:33].

## 4. Goorma ayaa HOD / LOD samaysmaa  [AUDIO+]
- **Bullish day:** Open → **Low** → High → Close. LOD-ka ayaa marka hore samaysma.
- **Bearish day:** Open → **High** → Low → Close. HOD-ka ayaa marka hore samaysma [P1 01:02–01:29].
- 6-da 4H candle, midkood ayaa waa inuu sameeyaa HOD ama LOD [P1 03:24–03:37, 04:38–04:44].
- **Inta badan:** HOD/LOD waxay ku samaysmaan **5 PM** ama **9 PM** (Asia). Mararka qaar **1 AM** (London) [P1 03:44–04:12].
- **9 AM (NY)** inta badan waa **continuation**, laakiin mararka qaar HOD/LOD ayuu sameeyaa [P1 04:15–04:29].

## 6. Purging Hours  [SLIDE + AUDIO+]
- **Qeexid [AUDIO+]:** Purging hours waa **confirmation**-ka in 4H candle-kaasi uu samayn doono **CRT**. Waa waqtiga ugu badan ee high-ga ama low-ga (manipulation) 4H candle-ka samaysmo [P1 07:45–08:07].
- **Tusaale:** 4H candle-ka 5 PM wuxuu ka kooban yahay saacadaha 5, 6, 7 iyo 8 PM. Low-ga (ama high-ga) wuxuu ku samaysmi karaa mid kasta, laakiin **inta badan 8 PM** [P1 08:15–08:44].

| Session | 4H candle | Purging hours (NY) |
|---|---|---|
| Pre Asian | 5 PM | **8 PM** |
| Asian | 9 PM | **9 PM, 10 PM** |
| London | 1 AM | **2 AM, 3 AM** |
| Pre NY | 5 AM | **6 AM, 8 AM** |
| NY | 9 AM | **9 AM, 10 AM** |

[P1 10:43–10:58] ayaa xaqiijinaya xiriirka 4H candle-ka iyo purging hours-ka.

## 7. Xiriirka casharrada kale
- **Casharka 04:** purging hours iyo CRT/AMD. Purging hour-ku waa waqtiga **manipulation**-ka (TS) ee CRT-ga 4H.
- Su'aalaha furan: midkoodna kuma jawaabin qaybtan.

## 8. Xiriirka indicator-ka (soo jeedin)
- **4H candle boundaries (NY time):** 5p/9p/1a/5a/9a/1p, oo session label leh.
- **Purging hour window** 4H candle kasta. TS-ka ku dhaca window-kaas wuxuu noqon karaa shuruud ama grade.
- **HOD/LOD tracking:** maalinta NY 5 PM ka bilaabanta, iyo 4H candle-ka uu ku samaysmay.
