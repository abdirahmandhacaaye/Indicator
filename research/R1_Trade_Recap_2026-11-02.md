## Waxa chart-yada muujinayaan  [CHART]
- **1H:** dhaqaaq bearish ah oo xoog leh, kadib low cusub ayaa la sameeyay, kadibna bounce.
- **7H:** candle 7H ah ayaa qaatay low-ga, kadib wick hoose ayuu sameeyay (sweep), oo ah 7H profile bullish.
- **5m:** range-ka Asia/London waxaa lagu sawiray lines (highs/lows). Low-ga range-ka waa la qaaday (TS), kadib **buy position** ayaa la galay: SL-ku wuxuu ku yaal sweep-ka hoostiisa (D1 ✅), TP-guna wuxuu ku yaal range high / ERL.
- Trade-ka labaad (5m, frame-ka u dambeeya): position box kale. Waxaa gacan lagu qoray **"-1%"** meel u dhow SL-ka, taas oo muujinaysa in trade ka mid ah uu SL ku dhacay (loss 1%). Tani waxay la jaanqaadaysaa risk 1% (casharka 16).
- **R:R** ee boxes-ka wuxuu u muuqdaa ~2–3R, taas oo la jaanqaadaysa D2 (3R).

Faahfaahinta (sababta entry-ga, sababta loss-ka) waxay ku jirtaa hadalka, waxaana lagu dari doonaa marka qoraalku dhammaado.
