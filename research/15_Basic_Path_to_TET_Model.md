## Waxa chart-ka ku qoran  [CHART]
- **"Putting the pieces together":** LIQUIDITY · ORDERFLOW · KILLZONES · KEY LEVELS. Waa afarta cashar ee Basic-ga oo isku xiran.
- Daily chart: **PDH** iyo **PDL** ayaa la sawiray (previous day high/low).
- 1H chart: PDH/PDL lines iyo swing lows/highs (liquidity). PDL-ka waa la qaaday (sweep), kadib suuqu kor ayuu u soo noqday. Tani waa **Turtle Soup ku dhaca PDL** (D CRT: candle-ka maanta wuxuu qaaday low-ga shalay, kadib reversal).

## Gunaanad
Casharku waa casharka u dambeeya ee Basic series-ka. Wuxuu isku xirayaa liquidity, order flow, killzones iyo key levels si loogu gudbo TET Model. Xeer cusub kuma jiro qaybtan. Waxay xaqiijinaysaa: **D CRT = PDH/PDL sweep → reversal** (casharrada 04, 19).
