## Waxa chart-ka ku qoran  [CHART]
- **"Putting the pieces together":** LIQUIDITY · ORDERFLOW · KILLZONES · KEY LEVELS. Waa afarta cashar ee Basic-ga oo isku xiran.
- Daily chart: **PDH** iyo **PDL** ayaa la sawiray (previous day high/low).
- 1H chart: PDH/PDL lines iyo swing lows/highs (liquidity). PDL-ka waa la qaaday (sweep), kadib suuqu kor ayuu u soo noqday. Tani waa **Turtle Soup ku dhaca PDL** (D CRT: candle-ka maanta wuxuu qaaday low-ga shalay, kadib reversal).

## Clip 2 (chart examples)  [CHART]
- **5m:** price wuxuu ku soo laabtay **FVG** (key level), wuxuu qaaday low-ga yar (**TS**), kadib wuxuu u kacay **ERL** iyo **PDH**. Position box: entry-ga kadib TS, TP-guna wuxuu ku yaal ERL.
- **1H → 5m:** **OB** iyo **KL** ayaa la calaamadeeyay, **ERL**-na wuxuu ahaa target-ka. 5m trade: SL-ka wuxuu ku yaal sweep-ka dushiisa, TP-guna wuxuu ku yaal ERL.
- **Chart-ka gunaanadka (1H):** TS → IRL → ERL → PDH → TS **9PM ASIAN** → KL → ERL → **10 AM** FVG → PDL → ERL → **PWL**. Tani waxay muujinaysaa socodka "liquidity to liquidity" iyo key times (9 PM Asia, 10 AM NY).
