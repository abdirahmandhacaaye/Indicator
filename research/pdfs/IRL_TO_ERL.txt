ERL – External Range Liquidity


ERL waa Liquidity (swing highs and swing lows ) ee lugta
dhaqaaqday ka so tagtay markii IRL la soo buuxinayay.


Waxaa inta badan ERL Noqda:


   Previous Day High (PDH)

   Previous Day Low (PDL)

   Previous Week High/Low

   Major Swing High/Low
