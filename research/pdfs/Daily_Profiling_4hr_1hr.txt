                         Daily Profiles
Daily Profiles waa mid kamid ah concept-yada ugu muhiimsan trading-ka.

sababtoo ah waxay kaa caawinayaan inaad fahanto sida market-ku u dhaqaaqo
maalin kasta iyo sida sessions ku isku raacaan . 



Suuqu si random ah uma socdo — Daily Profiles waxay trader-ka siinayaan
roadmap uu ku aqoonsado High of the Day (HOD), Low of the Day (LOD), direction-
ka maalinta, iyo sida u heli lahayd entries tayo sare leh
