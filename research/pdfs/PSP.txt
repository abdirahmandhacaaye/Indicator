Waa maxay Precision Swing Point (PSP)?



Precision Swing Point (PSP) waa kala duwanaansho ka
dhex dhaca xiritaanka candles-ka laba asset oo isku xiran
(correlated assets).



Marka laba asset oo xiriir leh ay sameeyaan qaab-
dhismeed isku mid ah, balse mid ka mid ah candle-ku ku
xirmo Bullish, halka kan kalena ku xirmo Bearish, kala
duwanaanshahaas ayaa loo yaqaan PSP.



Si la mid ah SMT divergence, PSP wuxuu diiradda saaraa
kala duwanaanshaha Qiimaha assets-ka.
