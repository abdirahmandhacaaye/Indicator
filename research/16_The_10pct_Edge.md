## 2. TRAPS  [SLIDE + AUDIO?]
Slide-yada waxay tusayaan setup-yada aan la qaadan.

| # | Trap | Waxa slide-ku muujinayo |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Inside Bar CRT** | Candle 2 ama in ka badan ayaa ku xirmay gudaha range-ka candle 1, iyagoo aan qaadin high-ga iyo low-ga [P1 13:07–13:19 AUDIO?: "high-ga ma qaadin, low-ga ma qaadin, labada liquidity way ka hareen"]. |
| 2 | **Range Candle (1H)** | Kadib displacement, candle yar oo range ah ayaa ku dhex samaysmay key level-ka (box-ka jaalaha). Calaamad "RANGE CANDLE" ayaa la saaray. Chart-ka labaad: suuqu wuxuu dhex maray range-ka oo sii socday. |
| 2b | **Exception** | **"However, if the key level has already formed a range, this rule no longer applies."** |
| 3 | **PDL to 1H TSQ: Retracement Phase** | "BEFORE": PDH, KL, #1 iyo PDL ayaa la calaamadeeyay, waxaana ku qoran **"DON'T SELL HERE"** oo ku yaal FVG-ga retracement-ka. "AFTER": suuqu kor ayuu u sii socday. |
| 4 | **Target reached** | **"Once the target is reached, we wait for the retracement before looking for another expansion."** |

- **Fasiraad (sugaya xaqiijinta qaybaha 2–4):**
  - **Trap 1:** Inside bar-ku ma aha CRT dhammaystiran. Waa in la sugo candle qaada high ama low-ga range-ka (TS), sida casharka 04 §2.5.
  - **Trap 2:** Range candle ku dhex jira key level-ka (accumulation yar) waa trap. Haddii key level-ku horay u lahaa range, xeerkani ma shaqeeyo. **[MA CADDA]** Faahfaahinta waxay u baahan tahay hadalka.
  - **Trap 3:** Kadib marka PDL (DOL) la qaado, dhaqaaqa kor u soo laabanaya waa **retracement**. Sell lagama galo key level-ka retracement-ka dhexdiisa ku jira. Tani waxay u muuqataa xeer **invalidation / no-trade** ah.
  - **Trap 4:** Marka target (DOL) la gaaro, setup cusub jihadaas looma raadiyo ilaa retracement la sameeyo. Tani waa **no-trade** rule.

## 4. Xiriirka indicator-ka (soo jeedin, waxay u baahan tahay ansixin)
- **No-trade filter A:** CRT range candle ha noqdo candle-kii xirmay oo leh TS (ma aha inside bar oo keliya).
- **No-trade filter B:** kadib marka DOL / target-ka la gaaro, signal cusub jihada isku midka ah lama bixiyo ilaa retracement (tusaale: qiimuhu ha gaaro key level ama 50% dealing range).
