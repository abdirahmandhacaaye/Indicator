## 1. Mawduuca

Casharku wuxuu ka kooban yahay laba qaybood:
- **2.0 IRL to ERL:** sida suuqu uga dhex dhaqaaqo liquidity-ga gudaha range-ka (IRL) iyo liquidity-ga ka baxsan (ERL).
- **3.0 Key Levels:** FVG, Order Block, IFVG, iyo key levels-ka timeframe-yada sare.

Tusaalooyinka chart-ku waa **XAUUSD (Gold), 1H, OANDA**, waxaana loo sameeyay TradingView Replay.

## 2. Fikradaha la baray

### 2.1 IRL — Internal Range Liquidity  [SLIDE]
> "IRL waa Key Level ku jira gudaha Dealing Range-ga suuqa. Badanaa waxay noqon kartaa: ORDERBLOCK, FVG, IFVG."

### 2.2 ERL — External Range Liquidity  [SLIDE]
> "ERL waa liquidity-ga yaalla bannaanka range-ga weyn. Tani waxay noqon kartaa: Previous Highs, Previous Lows, Major Swing Points, Buy-side ama Sell-side liquidity."

### 2.3 Dealing Range  [AUDIO?]
- Waa range-ka lugta (leg) suuqu ka samaysmay [02:41–02:52].
- Marka suuqu displacement ku sameeyo, wuxuu ka tagaa imbalance (FVG), oo macallinku ugu yeeray "footprints / raadadka". Suuqa ayaa dib ugu laabta imbalance-kaas (IRL), kadibna wuxuu bartilmaameedsadaa ERL [03:00–03:50].
- **[MA CADDA]** Sida saxda ah ee dealing range-ka loo sawiro: swing-kee laga bilaabo, iyo timeframe-kee.

### 2.4 Qaabka "IRL → ERL"  [SLIDE + AUDIO+]
- Diagram-ka slide-ka waxaa ku qoran: **IRL (Reversal)**, **FVG**, **ERL (Target)**.
- Macnaha: suuqu wuxuu dib ugu soo laabtaa IRL (FVG/OB) gudaha range-ka, halkaas ayuu ka reversal-gareeyaa, kadibna wuxuu u socdaa ERL (high/low hore).
- Hadalka [16:27–16:47]: "Marwalba internal range liquidity-ga la imaado (oo la ixtiraamo), target-ku waa external range liquidity."

### 2.5 Qaabka "ERL → IRL"  [SLIDE + AUDIO?]
- Diagram-ka slide-ka waxaa ku qoran: **ERL (Reversal)**, **IRL (Target)**, iyo calaamad gacan lagu qoray oo ah "DR" (waxay u badan tahay Dealing Range, laakiin ma hubo).
- Waa qaabka ka soo horjeeda: suuqu wuxuu qaataa ERL (liquidity-ga high/low), halkaas ayuu ka reversal-gareeyaa, kadibna wuxuu bartilmaameedsadaa IRL (FVG/OB) [05:24–05:45].

### 2.6 Major / HTF Key Levels  [AUDIO?]
- Key levels-ka timeframe-ka sare: **Daily, Weekly, Monthly** [06:15].
- Suuqa ayaa ka reversal-gareeya marka uu taabto HTF key level [06:20–06:37].
- **[MA CADDA]** Erayga "complex pullback" [06:24–06:28] iyo xiriirka uu la leeyahay HTF level-ka.

### 2.7 Key Level-ka koowaad: FVG (Fair Value Gap)  [SLIDE + AUDIO+]
- Waa 3 candle: candle 1, candle 2 iyo candle 3.
- Gap-ka waa meesha u dhaxaysa **wick-ka candle 1 iyo wick-ka candle 3**, oo ay candle 2 samaysay [08:55–09:49].
- Macallinku wuxuu FVG ku tilmaamay key level-ka ugu muhiimsan [08:46–08:47 "kan ugu horreeya / ugu muhiimsan"] [AUDIO?].
- Key level waa meesha suuqu ka sameeyo reaction/reversal, halkaasna laga raadiyo entry [09:49].

### 2.8 Key Level-ka labaad: Order Block  [SLIDE + AUDIO+]
- **Bearish OB:** marka lugtu bearish tahay, OB-gu waa **candle-kii u dambeeyay ee kor u socday (up-close)** ka hor intaan suuqu hoos u dhicin [10:37–10:58].
- **Bullish OB:** waa tan ka soo horjeeda. Waa **candle-kii u dambeeyay ee hoos u socday (down-close)** ka hor intaan suuqu kor u kicin [11:19–11:43].
- Sababta: institutions ayaa candle-kaas ku dhex leh orders, suuquna dib ayuu ugu soo laabtaa si orders-ka la buuxiyo [10:58–11:19] [AUDIO?].
- **[MA CADDA]** OB-ga waxaa lagu qeexay body-ga candle-ka mise high/low-giisa oo dhan? Slide-ku wuxuu muujinayaa line ka soo baxaya **open-ka** candle-ka. Ma jirto shuruud displacement ama FVG oo lagu xiray OB-ga.

### 2.9 Key Level-ka saddexaad: IFVG (Inverse FVG)  [SLIDE + AUDIO+]
- Waa FVG uu suuqu **"disrespect"** ku sameeyay, kadibna wuxuu noqdaa key level jihada ka soo horjeedda [11:52–13:50].
- **Respect:** suuqu wuxuu taabtaa FVG-ga oo ka soo laabtaa.
- **Disrespect:** suuqu wuu dhex jabiyaa oo dhaafaa.
- Slide-ka IFVG wuxuu muujinayaa bullish FVG la jabiyay, kadibna qiimuhu wuxuu u socdaa ERL hoose.
- **[MA CADDA]** Disrespect waa in candle uu ku **xirmo (close)** dhinaca kale ee FVG-ga mise wick ayaa ku filan? Hadalka lagama fahmi karo.

### 2.10 Calaamadaha chart-ka (XAUUSD 1H)  [SLIDE]
Calaamadaha lagu sawiray chart-yada:
- **ERL:** highs/lows-ka swing-yada.
- **IRL:** sanduuqyo (FVG) iyo lines.
- **TS**
- **DOL**

| Calaamad | Waxa la og yahay |
|---|---|
| **DOL** | Hadalka [15:37]: "ilaa DOL-ka la qaado". Waxay u badan tahay *Draw on Liquidity* [AUDIO?]. |
| **TS** | Lama sharxin. Chart-ka waxay ku taal **low-ga ugu hooseeya ee swing-ka** (bullish) ama **high-ga ugu sarreeya** (bearish), oo ah meesha lugtu ka bilaabmatay. **[MA CADDA]**, macnaha ha la xaqiijiyo. |

## 4. Waxa u baahan xaqiijintaada

1. **TS** macnaheedu waa maxay?
2. OB-ga ma waxaa lagu qeexaa body-ga candle-ka mise high/low-giisa oo dhan? Ma u baahan yahay displacement/FVG?
3. IFVG: disrespect ma waa candle **close** ka gudba FVG-ga?
4. Dealing range-ka sidee loo sawiraa: swing-kee iyo timeframe-kee?

## 5. Xiriirka indicator-ka (soo jeedin, lama ansixin)

Fikradahan waa la detect-gareyn karaa iyadoo aan lookahead la isticmaalin:
- **FVG:** 3 candle oo xiran.
- **OB:** candle-kii u dambeeyay ee jihada ka soo horjeeda, ka hor displacement.
- **IFVG:** FVG uu candle ku xirmay dhinaca kale.
- **ERL:** swing highs/lows, PDH/PDL, PWH/PWL.
- **"IRL respected → target ERL"** wuxuu noqon karaa shuruud signal.
