## 1. Key Levels — qeexid
> "Marka ICT laga hadlayo, Key Levels waa meelaha ugu muhiimsan ee uu qiimuhu (price) inta badan ka falceliyo. Waa zone/area muhiim ah oo halkaas suuqu ka samayn doono dhaqaaq kale.
> **Si fudud u faham:** Ka fikir waddo ay baabuur badan maraan. Meelaha bostejoyinka waa Key Levels. Halkaas ayaa gaadiidka lagu sugaa si aad u raacdid. Suuqa-na waa sidaas."

> "Suuqa marka uu expansion sameeyo, waxaa xiga retracement. Retracement-ku wuxuu ku dhammaadaa key levels, halkaas oo ah point-ga uu ka bilaabmi doono expansion-ka xiga."

## 2. Maxaa Key Levels muhiim ka dhigaya?

## 4. 4.2 Order Block (OB)
> "Order Block (OB) waa meesha ay institutions (bangiyada iyo hayadaha waaweyn) ka galiyeen suuqa orders badan, taas oo sababtay in price-ku si xoog leh u dhaqaaqo (**displacement**). Si fudud u faham: Order Block = Meeshii uu dhaqaaqii weynaa ka bilaabmay."

> "**Bullish OB:** Last bearish candle before bullish **displacement**. Waxaa laga raadiyaa: Buy Entry, Continuation, Reversal."

- **Muhiim (cusub):** OB-ga waxaa shuruud u ah **displacement**, oo ah dhaqaaq xoog leh oo ka dambeeya candle-ka. Tani waxay ka jawaabaysaa qayb ka mid ah Q6: OB-gu waa candle-ka u dambeeya ee ka horreeya displacement-ka.
- **Bearish OB:** "Last bullish candle before bearish displacement. Waxaa laga raadiyaa: Sell Entry, Continuation, Reversal."
- Slide-yada chart-ka: line-ka **"OB"** wuxuu ka bilaabmaa **open-ka (body edge)** candle-ka OB-ga. Bullish OB-ga waa body-ga sare ee last bearish candle, bearish OB-gana waa body-ga hoose ee last bullish candle. Tani waxay la jaanqaadaysaa line-ka **#1** ee casharka 07. → **Q6: OB level = open (body edge) ee candle-ka**, wick-gana uma dhererin. (SL-ku wuxuu ku yaal TS wick, D1.)

## 5. 4.3 Fair Value Gap (FVG)  [SLIDE]
> "Waa farqi u dhexeeya 3 candles. Price inta badan wuu ku soo noqdaa, fursadna waa laga raadsadaa."

- **Bullish FVG:** support zone, raadi buy. Box-ku wuxuu u dhexeeyaa **high-ga candle 1** iyo **low-ga candle 3**.
- **Bearish FVG:** resistance zone, raadi sell. Box-ku wuxuu u dhexeeyaa **low-ga candle 1** iyo **high-ga candle 3**.

## 6. 4.4 Inverse FVG (IFVG)  [SLIDE]
> "FVG markii role-kiisu is beddelo. Support → Resistance, Resistance → Support."

- Chart-yada: candle-ku wuxuu ku xirmaa (close) dhinaca kale ee FVG-ga, kadibna zone-ka ayaa noqda role ka soo horjeeda. (D5: IFVG laguma darayo indicator-ka.)

## 7. 4.5 Breaker Block  [SLIDE]
> "**Order Block la jabiyey.** Kadib wuxuu noqdaa support ama resistance cusub."

- Chart-yada (`img13/breaker_block_examples.png`): OB-ga ayaa la jabiyaa (price wuxuu ku xirmaa dhinaca kale), markuu dib ugu soo laabtana box-ka "BREAKER BLOCK" ayaa noqda support (bullish) ama resistance (bearish).
- **Breaker vs Mitigation Block [AUDIO?, P3 07:57–08:06]:** "tani waa breaker, tanina waa mitigation block... marka liquidity la qaado waa breaker". Fasiraad: OB la jabiyay **kadib liquidity sweep** = Breaker. Haddii sweep aan jirin = Mitigation Block (D5: laguma darayo).
- Xiriirka casharka 04: **BRK** ee The Sequence (CRTH + TS → #1 → **BRK** → OTE → KOD → CRTL).

## 8. 4.6 Gap  [SLIDE]
> "Gap waa aag uu price-ku si degdeg ah uga gudbay, taas oo sababtay ganacsi (volume) yar inuu ka dhaco aaggaas. Si fudud: Gap = Meel uu price-ku ku orday xawaare sare oo uusan waqti badan ku qaadan."

- (D5: Gap laguma darayo.)

## 9. Tusaalooyinka chart (P3–P4, XAUUSD 1H)  [SLIDE]
- Chart-yada waxaa lagu calaamadeeyay ERL, ERL/BSL, OB iyo FVG boxes. Socodku waa: dhaqaaq → key level (OB/FVG) → reaction → ERL. Faahfaahinta hadalka waxay ku xiran tahay transcript-ka (waa la sugayaa).

## 10. Xiriirka indicator-ka (soo jeedin)
- **OB detection:** candle-kii u dambeeyay ee jihada ka soo horjeeday, oo ay ku xigto **displacement** (tusaale: candle(s) ku xirma high/low-ga OB-ga dhinaca kale, ama FVG ka samaysma). Displacement-ka qeexiddiisa saxda ah (inta candle / xajmiga) ma jirto; soo jeedin: candle-ka xiga ha ku xirmo dhinaca kale ee OB-ga, ama FVG ha ka samaysmo.
- **OB level:** open-ka (body edge) candle-ka OB-ga (Q6).
- **FVG:** high candle 1 ↔ low candle 3 (bullish), low candle 1 ↔ high candle 3 (bearish).
- **Breaker:** OB uu candle ku xirmay dhinaca kale → role-ka ayaa is beddela.
