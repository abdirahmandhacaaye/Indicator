## 1. Daily bias (bog 2)
- **Manipulation relevant low:** daily candle ayaa qaatay **relevant low** (key level / FVG box), kadib dib ayuu ugu xirmay → **"Expecting buy next day"**.
- **Failed to manipulate relevant high:** suuqu wuxuu u socday hoos, kadib candle (C3) ayaa reversal sameeyay (**"c3 reversal"**) isagoo aan qaadin relevant high-ka → **"Target next relevant high"**.
- "Relevant swing" = swing highs/lows-ka muhiimka ah (bog 1).

## 2. Confirmation — 7H Profiling (bog 3)
> "Si aan bias-ka u confirm gareyno waxaan isticmaaleynaa daily profiles, waxaana u kala saareynaa 3 sessions. Waxaan isticmaaleynaa 7H timeframe. Session walba wuxuu ka kooban yahay 7 saacadood."

| Session | 7H candle (NY time) | Soomaaliya |
|---|---|---|
| **Asia** | **18:00** (furashada daily candle-ka) → 00:00 | 1 AM → 7 AM |
| **London** | **1 AM** → 7 AM | 8 AM → 2 PM |
| **NY** | **8 AM** → daily close | 3 PM → close |

> "Marka aan isticmaaleyno 7H profiling, waxaan focus-ka saareynaa **8am 7H NY candle**, oo ah session-ka ugu dhaqaaqa badan daily candle-ka."

## 4. Entries + Example 1 (bog 10–13)
- **Daily:** relevant high / relevant low; candle-ka maanta wuxuu qaatay relevant high (wick), kadib bearish. Daily **0.5** ayaa la sawiray.
- **7H:** 18:00, 1:00, 8:00 lines. Asia ayaa qaatay high-ga, London iyo NY waa bearish (Asia reversal → continuation).
- **1H:** 18:00 / 1:00 / 8AM lines. Swing high-ka ayaa la qaaday, kadib lower highs, 8AM-ka wuxuu sameeyay entry (sweep yar + breakdown), target-kuna waa relevant low.

## 5. Xiriirka TET
- Waa isla fikradda TET 7H model (D bias → 7H profile → 1H/5m entry), laakiin waxay ku daraysaa **qeexid cad oo la detect-gareyn karo** profile kasta:
  - Asia reversal = 1AM candle **ma qaadin** 18:00 high/low, kuna xirmay dibadda.
  - London reversal = 1AM **qaaday** 18:00 high/low, kadibna gudaha ku xirmay.
  - NY reversal = 18:00 iyo 1AM **reversal ma sameyn** (consolidation/retracement) → 8AM.
- Tani waa mid aad ugu habboon indicator-ka (non-repainting: 7H candles-ka xirmay).
