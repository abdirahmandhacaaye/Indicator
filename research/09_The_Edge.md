## Qaybta 1aad (00:00–15:34)  [AUDIO? + CHART]
- Casharku wuxuu tusayaa sida model-ka loogu isticmaalo **HTF ilaa LTF**: Weekly → Daily → 1H [P1 00:39–00:46].
- **Weekly:** SMT ayaa ka dhacday (XAUUSD vs XAUEUR / GBP), kadibna **weekly key level (FVG)** ayaa la ixtiraamay [P1 01:48–02:59].
- **Confirmation:** previous weekly high ayaa la qaaday, kadibna previous weekly low ayaa noqday **DOL** (target) [P1 03:07–03:32].
- **Daily-ga waa inuu la jaanqaadaa** weekly-ga: daily key level, daily CRT [P1 03:45–04:33]. "Weekly CRT-ga waxaa ku dhex jira daily CRT."
- **Targets:** daily CRT low = target-ka koowaad, DOL = target-ka labaad [P1 04:33–04:41].
- **1H:** previous weekly high ayaa la qaaday, candle-ka **8 PM**-na entry ayuu bixiyay. Order flow-gu waa isbeddelay, target-kuna waa daily CRT low [P1 05:01–05:25].
- Kadib TSQ (The Sequence) iyo Model #1 [P1 05:32].

**Gunaanad:** casharku ma soo kordhinayo xeer cusub. Wuxuu isku daraa xeerarkii hore (SMT, key level, CRT, TS, purging hour 8 PM, Model #1, DOL) oo wuxuu ku dabaqayaa chart dhab ah. Qaybaha kale waxaa lagu dari doonaa marka qoraalkooda dhammaado, haddii ay wax cusub ku jiraan.
